The Ritual of Churrasco

How the Experience Works

A continuous, guest-controlled dining ritual rooted in the traditions of Southern Brazil.

Your Pace, Your Plate

The Green & Red Coaster

This simple tool puts you in complete control of the flow of fire-roasted meats.

Green
Green side up
Gaúchos keep carving
Red
Red side up
Service pauses for you
1

Arrive & Settle

You’re seated and given the iconic coaster. Start with drinks or head straight to the Market Table while the fire is ready.

2

Market Table First

Build your plate from seasonal salads, cheeses, charcuterie, pickled vegetables, hot Brazilian sides (rice, black beans, farofa), and more. Return as often as you like.

3

Flip to Green

Place the green side up. Gaúcho chefs appear with skewers of picanha, ribeye, filet, lamb, pork, chicken, sausage, and grilled pineapple. They carve tableside to your preference.

4

Pause Anytime

Turn the coaster to red when you want a break. Enjoy conversation, return to the Market Table, or order another round from the bar. Flip back to green when you’re ready for more.

Tips for First-Timers

Make the Most of Your Visit

Start Light at the Market

Don’t fill up too early. Sample the Market Table, then let the meats take center stage.

Try Everything Once

Picanha is the star, but don’t miss fraldinha, lamb, or the grilled pineapple with cinnamon.

Pace Yourself

Use the red side freely. This is a marathon, not a sprint — the best cuts keep coming.

Reservations Recommended

Especially on weekends and for larger groups. Walk-ins are welcome when space allows.
