The Ritual

How the Rodízio Works

A continuous tableside experience guided by a simple coaster. Green means more fire. Red means pause.

Green
Keep the cuts coming
Red
Pause & enjoy
1

Market Table

Begin at our Gourmet Hot & Cold Market Table — seasonal salads, cheeses, charcuterie, and authentic Brazilian sides. Unlimited visits.

2

Tableside Carving

Gaúcho chefs circulate with skewers of fire-roasted prime cuts. Flip your coaster to green and they carve to your plate.

3

Your Pace

Turn the coaster red anytime to pause. Savor, talk, return to the Market Table, or order a caipirinha. The experience is yours.

4

Finish Strong

End with Brazilian desserts, handcrafted cocktails, or a glass from our curated wine cellar.
