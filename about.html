A Heritage Reimagined

The Meaning of Fogão

In the heart of the Brazilian pampas, the fogão — the traditional hearth — was more than a stove. It was the pulse of the home, a source of warmth where stories were shared and the finest meats were slow-roasted over open flames.

At Fogão Gaúcho, we have brought that ancient spirit to California, elevating the rugged art of churrasco with an atmosphere of grace. Here, the raw power of the fire meets the refined touch of white tablecloths and polished silver.

We believe great food and genuine hospitality create connection. Our restaurants are designed for the people of our community — intimate settings where celebrations feel personal and every guest is treated like family.

Quality is a Quiet Obsession

We don’t just order ingredients. We source with a chef’s eye for perfection — prime cuts, seasonal produce for the Market Table, and a carefully curated wine list that complements the fire.

Our gaúcho chefs are trained in the traditional techniques of tableside carving, ensuring every slice arrives at its peak.

Our Philosophy

Fire First

Everything begins with the open flame. We honor the traditional method of roasting high-quality cuts simply, so the meat speaks for itself.

Guest in Control

The coaster system puts the pace of the meal entirely in your hands. Green for more, red for pause — hospitality that listens.

Community Table

We are family-owned and community-focused. Whether it’s a quiet Tuesday or a milestone celebration, every table matters.

Elevated Tradition

Rugged churrasco deserves white tablecloths and soulful atmosphere. We bridge the farm hearth with refined dining.
